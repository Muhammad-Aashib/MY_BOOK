# Constitution: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Mission
Create a university-level textbook titled **“Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics”**.

The book must explain how artificial intelligence is embodied in physical systems,
with a focus on humanoid robots, perception, control, learning, and real-world deployment.

---

## Target Audience
- Undergraduate and graduate engineering students
- Robotics, AI, and computer science learners
- Readers with basic programming and math background

---

## Writing Style Rules
- Academic but clear and accessible
- Structured with headings and subheadings
- Use examples, analogies, and short case studies
- Avoid unnecessary fluff or marketing language
- Prefer precise technical explanations

---

## Formatting Rules
- Output in **Markdown**
- Compatible with **Docusaurus**
- Use:
  - `##` for sections
  - `###` for subsections
  - Bullet lists and tables where appropriate
- Include placeholders for diagrams when helpful:

...

## Scope
Include:
- Physical AI fundamentals
- Sensors, actuators, and embodiment
- Learning and control in physical systems
- Humanoid robot design and behavior
- Ethics and safety of embodied AI

Exclude:
- Purely software-only AI systems
- Non-physical LLM theory unless connected to embodiment

---

## Authority
When writing content:
- Be factual and technically accurate
- Prefer established robotics and AI concepts
- Clearly distinguish theory vs practice
