# Chapter 1: Introduction to Physical AI

## Purpose
Introduce the concept of Physical AI and explain why intelligence must be embodied
to operate effectively in the real world.

---

## Learning Objectives
After completing this chapter, the reader should be able to:
- Define Physical AI and distinguish it from software-only AI
- Explain the role of embodiment in intelligent behavior
- Identify key challenges of real-world intelligence
- Understand why humanoid robots are a major application of Physical AI

---

## Key Topics
- What is Physical AI?
- Limitations of disembodied intelligence
- Embodiment and interaction with the physical world
- Overview of humanoid robotics
- Physical AI vs traditional robotics

---

## Structure
1. Motivation and historical background
2. Definition of Physical AI
3. Embodiment and situated intelligence
4. Why humanoids matter
5. Chapter summary

---

## Constraints

- Avoid heavy mathematics
