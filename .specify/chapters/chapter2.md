# Chapter 2: Embodiment and Intelligence

## Purpose
Explain why embodiment is central to intelligent behavior and how physical bodies
shape perception, cognition, and action in both biological and artificial systems.

---

## Learning Objectives
After completing this chapter, the reader should be able to:
- Explain the concept of embodied intelligence
- Describe how physical form influences cognition
- Understand sensorimotor coupling
- Relate embodiment theories to Physical AI systems

---

## Key Topics
- Embodied cognition
- Sensorimotor loops
- Morphological computation
- Body–environment coupling
- Comparison with disembodied AI

---

## Structure
1. Historical views of intelligence
2. Embodied cognition theory
3. Sensorimotor coupling
4. Morphological computation
5. Implications for Physical AI design
6. Chapter summary
